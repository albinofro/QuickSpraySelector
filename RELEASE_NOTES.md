# QuickSpraySelector 1.0.0

Created by **Stooo**. First public release.

Replaces the Graffiti Minigame with a weapon wheel style selector. Press cancel to spray last used piece.

- Browse your collected graffiti with a large artwork preview in the middle.
- Choose with either analog stick, the mouse, D-pad or keyboard; button helpers follow the game's bindings and input device.
- Recall the last successfully sprayed artwork separately for each size.
- Keep the native finishing spray, artwork reveal and rewards.
- Enjoy flowing paint styling, native character poses and yellow paint effects, and randomized spray-can lid sounds capped at three voices.
- Keep foreground dots sharp while delayed background blur and camera motion add depth. Blur quality is configurable.

The screen may hang for a second on the first spray after launching the game.

Install the Thunderstore package through your mod manager, or copy its plugin folder into BepInEx/plugins. Requires BepInEx for Bomb Rush Cyberfunk.
